Complete Curriculum

15-Module Beginner Freight Dispatcher Course

01Introduction to Freight Dispatching+

An overview of what a freight dispatcher does and how dispatching fits into the trucking industry.

02Understanding the Trucking Industry+

A look at the roles, terminology, and structure of the trucking industry.

03Trucks, Trailers & Equipment+

An introduction to common truck and trailer types used in freight transportation.

04Carrier Onboarding & Required Documents+

What's involved in setting up a carrier profile and the documents typically required.

05Load Boards & Finding Loads+

How load boards work and how dispatchers search for available loads.

06Rate Per Mile & Load Calculations+

How rate-per-mile and load-related calculations are used in dispatching.

07Calling Freight Brokers+

How dispatchers communicate with freight brokers about available loads.

08Rate Negotiation+

Approaches to negotiating rates with brokers on a carrier's behalf.

09Booking the Load & Rate Confirmations+

What happens when a load is booked and how rate confirmations are handled.

10Managing an Active Load+

What's involved in tracking and managing a load once it's in progress.

11BOL, Delivery & POD+

An introduction to bills of lading, delivery, and proof-of-delivery documentation.

12Factoring, Invoicing & Payments+

An overview of factoring, invoicing, and how carriers are typically paid.

13Reefer, Flatbed, Box Truck & Hotshot Dispatching+

Dispatching considerations specific to reefer, flatbed, box truck, and hotshot equipment.

14Building a Carrier's Week & Lane Planning+

How dispatchers help plan a carrier's week and preferred lanes.

15Starting & Operating a Dispatch Business+

An introduction to what's involved in starting and operating a dispatch business.
