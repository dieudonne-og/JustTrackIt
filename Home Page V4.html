FAQ

Common Questions

What does a freight dispatcher do?+

A freight dispatcher searches for loads, negotiates rates with brokers, handles broker communication, and helps with paperwork and route planning — so you can focus on driving.

How does the dispatching process work?+

It starts with a consultation, followed by setting up your carrier profile and dispatch agreement. From there, your dispatcher searches for loads and communicates with brokers on your behalf, and you decide which loads to run.

Do I have to accept every load?+

No. Just Truck It operates on a no-forced-dispatch basis. You make the final decision on every load.

How do I speak with someone about dispatch services?+

Call 912-546-1923, email justtruckitdispatch@gmail.com, or book a consultation directly through the website.
