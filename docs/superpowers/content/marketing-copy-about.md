## The Story Behind Just Truck It

Eyebrow: Behind The Business

H2: The Story Behind Just Truck It

**01 — Her Story** (How she got into freight dispatching.)
> "I was looking for something different—something I could learn, grow in, and eventually turn into a business of my own."

That search led her to freight dispatching. She began learning about the trucking industry, load boards, brokers, carriers, rate negotiation, and everything that goes into keeping a truck moving. The more she learned, the more she realized this was something she could actually do—and build on.

**02 — Why Just Truck It** (The story behind starting the business.)
> "I didn't want to just learn dispatching—I wanted to build something that belonged to me."

That mindset became the foundation for Just Truck It Dispatching: taking what she was learning and turning it into a business of her own, built around supporting carriers and helping keep their trucks moving.

**03 — Why She Teaches** (Why she created Just Truck It Dispatch Academy.)
> "When I started learning dispatching, I realized there is SO much information out there. And as a beginner, sometimes you're sitting there thinking, 'Okay, but where do I actually start?'"

That experience inspired Just Truck It Dispatch Academy. She wanted to create the type of training she would have wanted when she first started—something beginner-friendly, step-by-step, and easy to understand.
