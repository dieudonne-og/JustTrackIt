## What Just Truck It Handles

Eyebrow: Dispatch Services

H2: What Just Truck It Handles

1. **Load Searching & Booking** — We search available loads that match your equipment and preferred lanes, then handle the booking process.
2. **Rate Negotiation** — We negotiate rates directly with brokers on your behalf before a load is confirmed.
3. **Broker Communication** — We communicate with brokers throughout the load process so you can spend less time on the phone.
4. **Paperwork Assistance** — We help keep your rate confirmations, load documents, and related paperwork organized.
5. **Route & Load Planning** — We help plan routes and loads around your preferences and business needs.
6. **Personalized Dispatch Support** — We work with you based on your preferences, lanes, equipment, and business goals.

## Getting Started With Dispatch

Eyebrow: How It Works

H2: Getting Started With Dispatch

1. **Sign Up** — You provide basic business, equipment, lane, and dispatching information.
2. **Complete Onboarding** — We complete your consultation, dispatch agreement, required documents, and authority/insurance verification.
3. **We Search & Negotiate** — We search for loads based on your preferences, evaluate load details, and negotiate with brokers.
4. **You Approve. We Dispatch.** — You make the final decision on every load. Once approved, we help coordinate the dispatch.

## Common Questions

Eyebrow: FAQ

H2: Common Questions

1. **How much does dispatch service cost?** Just Truck It charges 10% of the load revenue, according to the dispatch agreement.
2. **Where do you provide dispatch services?** Nationwide.
3. **What equipment do you dispatch?** Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Box Truck, Hotshot, and Power Only.
4. **Do I have to accept every load?** No. There is no forced dispatch. You make the final decision on every load.
5. **How do I get started?** Start with our sign-up and onboarding process. Just Truck It also offers a free 15-minute carrier consultation to talk through what you're looking for.
