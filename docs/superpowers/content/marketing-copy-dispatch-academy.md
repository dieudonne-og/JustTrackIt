## 15-Module Beginner Freight Dispatcher Course (Complete Curriculum)

Eyebrow: Complete Curriculum

H2: 15-Module Beginner Freight Dispatcher Course

01. **Introduction to Freight Dispatching** — An overview of what a freight dispatcher does and how dispatching fits into the trucking industry.
02. **Understanding the Trucking Industry** — A look at the roles, terminology, and structure of the trucking industry.
03. **Trucks, Trailers & Equipment** — An introduction to common truck and trailer types used in freight transportation.
04. **Carrier Onboarding & Required Documents** — What's involved in setting up a carrier profile and the documents typically required.
05. **Load Boards & Finding Loads** — How load boards work and how dispatchers search for available loads.
06. **Rate Per Mile & Load Calculations** — How rate-per-mile and load-related calculations are used in dispatching.
07. **Calling Freight Brokers** — How dispatchers communicate with freight brokers about available loads.
08. **Rate Negotiation** — Approaches to negotiating rates with brokers on a carrier's behalf.
09. **Booking the Load & Rate Confirmations** — What happens when a load is booked and how rate confirmations are handled.
10. **Managing an Active Load** — What's involved in tracking and managing a load once it's in progress.
11. **BOL, Delivery & POD** — An introduction to bills of lading, delivery, and proof-of-delivery documentation.
12. **Factoring, Invoicing & Payments** — An overview of factoring, invoicing, and how carriers are typically paid.
13. **Reefer, Flatbed, Box Truck & Hotshot Dispatching** — Dispatching considerations specific to reefer, flatbed, box truck, and hotshot equipment.
14. **Building a Carrier's Week & Lane Planning** — How dispatchers help plan a carrier's week and preferred lanes.
15. **Starting & Operating a Dispatch Business** — An introduction to what's involved in starting and operating a dispatch business.
