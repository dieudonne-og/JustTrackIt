What Just Truck It Handles

Load Searching & Booking

We search available loads that match your equipment and preferred lanes, then handle the booking process.

Rate Negotiation

We negotiate rates directly with brokers on your behalf before a load is confirmed.

Broker Communication

We communicate with brokers throughout the load process so you can spend less time on the phone.

Paperwork Assistance

We help keep your rate confirmations, load documents, and related paperwork organized.

Route & Load Planning

We help plan routes and loads around your preferences and business needs.

Personalized Dispatch Support

We work with you based on your preferences, lanes, equipment, and business goals.

FAQ

Common Questions

How much does dispatch service cost?+

Just Truck It charges 10% of the load revenue, according to the dispatch agreement.

Where do you provide dispatch services?+

Nationwide.

What equipment do you dispatch?+

Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Box Truck, Hotshot, and Power Only.

Do I have to accept every load?+

No. There is no forced dispatch. You make the final decision on every load.
